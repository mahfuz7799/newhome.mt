About us

At NewHome Developments, we are a family-owned property development business in Malta, built on professional expertise and quality workmanship. For the past six years, we have combined our backgrounds in architecture, engineering and project management to deliver carefully planned and professionally managed residential developments.

As brothers and business partners, we take a hands-on approach to every project, bringing together technical knowledge, practical experience and close attention to detail. From the initial planning stages to the final finishes, we oversee the work personally to ensure that each stage meets our standards for quality, organisation and delivery.

We believe that a successful development depends as much on communication and trust as it does on construction. Our clients have direct contact with us throughout the project and after completion, providing a clear point of contact for questions, updates and ongoing support. We take our commitments seriously and work closely with our teams to maintain progress and meet agreed timeframes.

We work with experienced tradespeople who hold the necessary licences and share our commitment to quality workmanship and a high standard of finish. By coordinating each trade and paying attention to the details, we aim to create homes that are well built and comfortable to live in.

Our involvement continues through to a smooth handover for new homeowners and the condominium management company. We take care to support this transition and remain available after completion, helping owners settle into their new homes.
